# Getting Started with the Project Management Website

Welcome to our AI-powered project management system. Here’s a quick overview to help you get started:

## What You Can Do:
- **Create Projects:** Organize your work by creating projects for each client or campaign.
- **Add Workflows:** Within each project, you can add workflows like strategy, design, publishing, etc.
- **Assign Tasks:** Add tasks under workflows and assign them to teammates.
- **Track Progress:** View task status updates, performance metrics, and event logs.
- **Use Brains:** Attach an AI “brain” to your project that can answer questions, summarize campaigns, and retrieve documents.

## Key Pages:
- **Dashboard:** Shows an overview of your projects and tasks.
- **Brains Page:** Where you create AI brains for semantic memory.
- **Workflow Canvas:** A visual interface to connect and organize your tasks.
